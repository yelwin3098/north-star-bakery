Our Story

North Star Bakery is an illustrative bakery concept created for a web development assignment. The story of a neighborhood team gathering around a shared oven is fictional sample content, designed to show how a small business can introduce its purpose online.

Ingredient Sourcing

Our sample sourcing philosophy is to choose dependable ingredients, reduce unnecessary packaging, and highlight seasonal flavors when they are available. Specific supplier relationships are not represented on this demonstration site.

Staff Highlights

  • The baker: an illustrative team member who develops breads and laminated pastries.
  • The cake lead: an illustrative team member who plans celebration cakes and seasonal decorations.
  • The counter host: an illustrative team member who welcomes visitors and helps with pickup questions.

These roles and descriptions are fictional sample content, not claims about a real business.
